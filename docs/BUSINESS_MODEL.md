# Free and open-source project

**DATE:** 2026-09-18

PassiveBuilder is free and open source, a foundation in the way OpenPH is. Core authoring and
export require no hosted application, database service, account, subscription or paid library
access. BLDGTYP sells no commercial license for it. The repository license is AGPL-3.0-or-later;
see [licensing](LICENSING.md) for what that means for products that connect to it or build on it.

The immediate value is better Phius project work at BLDGTYP and other consulting firms. The
longer value is a community front end for METr that no single firm has to carry. The METr team
has said they will not build a graphical front end and welcomes an open one. Development,
maintenance, compatibility testing, support and distribution still require effort, and internal
use is the practical basis for prioritizing that work until contributors arrive.

## Optional libraries

A firm, a vendor or METr itself may publish definitions through a library provider. The useful
capability is the provider contract and the import tool, not any one catalog. Licensed source
material is not redistributed as part of this repository. Library rights and provenance depend on
the actual source and its permitted use.

## Sibling and downstream projects

PassivSure may reuse the UI kit under the repository license for its own PHI-focused product.
METr's business arrangements are its own. No revenue funnel, subscription conversion or platform
milestone controls this product's roadmap.
