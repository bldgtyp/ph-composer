# Generic starter Materials

Twelve editable examples for learning the library-file workflow. Add `materials.json` through
Library setup. The file is separate from the extension and is excluded from its RBZ.

These are round representative values, not current product declarations or certification inputs.
Replace them with the applicable project evidence. Blank density, specific heat and emissivity
mean unknown; they do not mean zero.

The insulation conductivities are our rounded SI conversions of public DOE nominal guidance.
For R-value per inch, conductivity is `0.1442279 / (R/in)` W/(m K). The first six examples use
[DOE/PNNL guidance, printed p. 7.5](https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/2013/11/f5/40percent_mixed_humid.pdf).
The foam examples use [DOE's 2010 guide, p. 1](https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/guide_to_home_insulation.pdf).
The guidance does not specify temperature, aging or blowing-agent conditions.

The gypsum example uses rounded means of four original NIST room-temperature measurements,
with paper in place: conductivity 0.28 W/(m K), density 750 kg/m³ and specific heat 1000 J/(kg K).
See [the experiment, printed p. 660](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=900117).
Specific heat follows Table 1, whose range differs from the adjacent prose.
